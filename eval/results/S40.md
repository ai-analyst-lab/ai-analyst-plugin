**Capability:** Meta — "pull up the numbers behind the MRR board": Session pre-flight resolves the glossary shorthand, reports the resolution, and the definition drives the analysis.
**Verdict:** PASS  **Grade:** B

## What the skill promised

analyst-core, Session pre-flight step 1: "Resolve shorthand against the org glossary or entity index (`.knowledge/organizations/{org}/entity-index.yaml`, **or the org's glossary, products, metrics, and teams files** …). Scan the question for known aliases, case-insensitive, whole-word, longest alias first so substrings do not collide. If matches are found, note them for the user: `Resolved: 'cvr' -> conversion_rate (metric)`."

## What actually happened

Setup: org glossary bootstrapped at `.knowledge/organizations/acme-saas/business/glossary/terms.yaml` (the layout the business skill defines) with "MRR board" defined as the Monthly Revenue Board — net order revenue by calendar month, refunded orders excluded.

Fresh session, prompt "pull up the numbers behind the MRR board":
1. `{org}` resolved via knowledge-bootstrap Step 5's chain (setup-state.yaml absent → dataset manifest's `organization: acme-saas`).
2. `entity-index.yaml` does not exist (nothing in the plugin creates one) → fell back to the glossary files, as the skill's "or" clause allows.
3. Longest-alias-first whole-word scan matched `'the MRR board'` (alias, 13 chars) before the bare term, exactly avoiding the substring-collision the skill warns about. Resolution reported before any analysis: `Resolved: 'the MRR board' -> MRR board (report): …`.
4. The glossary definition then **shaped the query**: monthly grouping, `refund_flag = 0` exclusion — converging with CORR-001 from the corrections store. Deliverable `outputs/s40_mrr_board_numbers.md`: $1,968.69 / $3,525.79 / $2,524.43 (Aug partial), with trace and Checks.

The shorthand was resolved and reported before analysis, and the resolution changed what was computed (net-by-month, not a guess at "MRR"). Promise delivered.

## Defects

1. **MISSING-PIECE (minor):** `entity-index.yaml` is the pre-flight's named primary path, but no skill creates it and no schema for it exists anywhere in the plugin (it appears exactly once, in analyst-core). Every real store will take the fallback branch; the primary branch is decorative until something writes that file.
2. **CONTRADICTION (minor):** docs/KNOWLEDGE.md declares itself "the single definition of that tree. Skills read and write only what is listed here" — yet `organizations/` (and `user/`, `query-archaeology/`, `setup-state.yaml`) are absent from that contract while analyst-core, business, and knowledge-bootstrap all read them. The bootstrap for this eval had to take the org layout from the business skill, not from the contract that claims authority.
3. **AMBIGUOUS (minor):** analyst-core's pre-flight never says how `{org}` is found; the resolution chain lives only in knowledge-bootstrap Step 5. Worked here because bootstrap was read too.
